The Ence Marketing Education Foundation’s mission is to educate
and help 1,000 non-profits, social enterprises, micro business and
small and medium enterprises (SMEs) to reach marketing
sustainability by executing campaigns effectively.

Our goal is to help organisations cultivate a clearly defined
marketing plan suitable for their needs.

We aim to help organisations flourish with proper marketing.

Through a multi-channel approach in the areas of public relations,
social media marketing, digital marketing, events, digital marketing,
an integrated marketing plan is often the best approach for effective
marketing for any organisation.
